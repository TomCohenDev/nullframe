# Diaphragma — AI Art Style Guide

## The Visual Identity

Mothership and WLOP sit at opposite ends — Mothership is harsh, high-contrast, industrial black and white. WLOP is luminous, painterly, and soft. The combination that works for Diaphragma:

> **Dark, atmospheric environments. Luminous, detailed characters. Cinematic tension.**

The environment carries the Mothership weight. The character carries the WLOP quality. Light becomes the storytelling device between them.


---

## Key Style Terms

| Term | What it does |
|---|---|
| `WLOP` | Painterly quality, luminous characters, detailed cloth and hair |
| `single dramatic light source` | Stops the image feeling flat — forces cinematic shadow |
| `muted desaturated background` | Pulls from Mothership's grey palette without going full black and white |
| `warm focal highlights` | WLOP's signature glow on the character or subject |
| `volumetric fog` | Depth and atmosphere — makes environments feel dangerous |
| `gritty industrial environment` | Grounds it in Mothership's tone |


### Sci-Fi Horror / Rimaer
```
a lone figure in a damaged vac-suit drifting through a derelict corridor, emergency red lighting, debris floating, something moving in the dark behind them
```

### Heist / Modern
```
a sharp-dressed fixer leaning against a rain-slicked alley wall at night, neon reflections, cigarette, city lights blurred behind them
```

```
a sun-cracked desert warrior beneath a dying red sun, bone armour, cracked earth stretching to the horizon, heat haze
```

## Negative Prompts


---

## Aspect Ratios

| Use | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Character portrait | `--ar 2:3` |
| Scene / environment | `--ar 16:9` |
| Book interior spot art | `--ar 1:1` or `--ar 3:4` |
| Full page spread | `--ar 3:4` |

---

## The Core Rule

> WLOP handles the light and the person. Mothership handles everything around them.
